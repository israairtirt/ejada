01/About us

Who we are

EJADA is an independent Libyan company, founded by a team with more than 10 years of combined experience in consulting and training. We specialise in organisational development (OD) and human resources (HR) advisory, alongside training, capacity building and recruitment services.

We support individuals and institutions through professional services grounded in practical experience, a deep understanding of local context, and a participatory approach that delivers results which are both actionable and sustainable. We believe people are the true engine of change — and we develop them through high-impact programmes and initiatives that build more effective organisations and more resilient communities.

Why EJADA?

We work with passion to be a strategic partner in empowering people and institutions — through tailored solutions that respect local context, rely on flexible methodologies, and combine technical knowledge with a deep understanding of the psychological, cultural and social environment.

With broad practical experience and a multidisciplinary team, EJADA seeks to make a real and lasting difference across the Libyan and regional workplace.

02/How we work

Our approach

At EJADA we do not believe in off-the-shelf solutions. Every institution and every individual has a unique need that deserves a carefully designed response.

  1. 1

    Listen & Understand

    We begin by creating a safe space to understand the context and its challenges — organisational, psychological or environmental.

  2. 2

    Analyse & Assess

    We analyse gaps, needs and strengths using both quantitative and qualitative tools.

  3. 3

    Design for Reality

    We craft practical solutions designed carefully around the context.

  4. 4

    Deliver Together

    We work side by side with the client, because partnership is the core of any successful intervention.

  5. 5

    Follow Up & Evaluate

    Our partnership does not stop at delivery — we review, measure impact and support real, sustainable change.

What sets EJADA apart

We combine technical and functional expertise with a deep reading of psychological, cultural and environmental factors. We do not analyse policies alone — we look at intrinsic motivation, psychological climate, relationships and the sense of safety inside teams.

Because real institutional change starts from within, and needs a human environment to support it.
